Our Heritage • Est. 1928

Slow craft in an age of disposable fast fashion.

Founded in 1928 along the historic docks of Copenhagen by master tailors Olav Lindström and Jack Hesselberg, our haberdashery remains dedicated to one unyielding principle: tailoring tools and garment notions should last a lifetime.

Every pair of shears is hand-ground on wet sandstone wheels in Eskilstuna; each horn button is turned individually to preserve its natural grain; and our wool threads are spun from hardy valley sheep that have grazed Scandinavian highlands for generations.

Ethical Sourcing

Natural horn, bone, shell, and timber gathered exclusively under strict Nordic ecological quotas.

Repair & Restoration

Send your shears back to our Copenhagen workshop anytime for complementary edge regrinding and recalibration.
